🛒 Amazon Superstore Sales & Profit Analysis | Power BI

📌 Project Overview                                                       This project presents an interactive **Amazon Superstore Sales & Profit Analysis Dashboard developed using Power BI**. The objective of the project was to analyze sales performance, profitability, product performance, customer segments, returns, and geographical profitability using the Superstore dataset.

The dashboard transforms raw sales data into meaningful **KPIs, visualizations, and business insights**, allowing users to quickly understand overall performance and identify profitable customers, products, categories, sub-categories, and cities.


🎯 Project Objectives
                                     The main objectives of this project were to:

  * Analyze **Total Sales** and overall revenue performance.
  * Analyze **Total Profit** to understand business profitability.
  * Track **Total Quantity** of products sold.
  * Analyze **Total Returns** and returned orders.
  * Compare **Sales by Customer Segment**.
  * Analyze **Sales by Product Category**.
  * Identify customers generating the highest profit.
  * Identify the most profitable products.
  * Analyze profit performance by sub-category.
  * Analyze **Sum of Profit by City**.
  * Present the analysis through an interactive Power BI dashboard.


📊 Dashboard Analysis

   💰 Total Sales:-
           Analyzed the total sales generated across the business to understand overall revenue performance.

   💵 Total Profit:-
       Analyzed total profit to evaluate the financial performance and overall profitability of the business.

   📦 Total Quantity:-
        Analyzed the total quantity of products sold to understand sales volume and product demand.

   🔄 Total Returns:-
         Analyzed total returns to understand returned orders and provide an additional perspective on overall business performance.

   👥 Sales by Segment:-
       Compared sales across different customer segments to identify which segments contribute the most to total sales.

  🏷️ Sales by Category:-
        Analyzed sales across different product categories to identify high-performing categories and their contribution to overall sales.

  👤 Profit by Customer:- 
        Analyzed profit generated by individual customers to identify high-value customers who contribute significantly to overall profitability.

  📦 Profit by Product:-
        Analyzed product-level profitability to identify the products generating the highest profit.

  📊 Profit by Sub-Category:-
     Compared profit across different sub-categories to identify highly profitable and less profitable areas of the product portfolio.

   🌎 Profit by City :- 
        Analyzed the **sum of profit by city** to identify cities contributing significantly to overall profitability and understand geographical differences in performance.


🔍 Key Insights
   The dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of the business by combining **sales, profit, quantity, returns, customer, product, category, sub-category, and city-level analysis**.

   The **Sales by Segment** analysis helps identify the customer segments contributing the most to revenue, while **Sales by Category** provides an overview of the strongest-performing product categories.

   The  **Profit by Customer** and **Profit by Product** analyses provide a deeper understanding of where the business generates the most profit. These analyses help identify high-profit customers and products.

   The **Profit by Sub-Category** analysis helps compare the profitability of different product groups and identify areas performing strongly or requiring further attention.

   The **Profit by City** analysis provides a geographical perspective of profitability and helps identify cities that contribute significantly to overall business profit.

   The inclusion of **Total Returns** allows business performance to be viewed alongside returned orders, providing a more complete understanding of sales activity.
